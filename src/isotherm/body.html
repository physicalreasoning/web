An open, Jev-style decision model: typed questions in, calibrated probabilities out, all read off one distribution over tomorrow's high. Every chart here is scored out of sample against the market's own price at the same timestamp. Code and raw results: physicalreasoning/isotherm. Ask it yourself: API or MCP for agents. results/transformer_large_gate.json · data_scaling.json · nbm_spread.json · explore_*.json

The market alone: mostly a pricing artefact

Market-only signals, log score gain over the mid-price market, test rows from 2026, 95% date-block CI (§39, exploration). Raising each bucket's price to a power of about 1.17 beats the mid market, but pricing buckets at their bids does most of that with no parameters: the mid counts unbid asks as probability (§41). Beyond the bid-priced market, sharpening wins only at 2pm. Sealed test against it from 2026-10-10.

results/alpha/weather_signals.json

Beyond weather: free prices do not beat the book

Benchmark v1, 13 Kalshi series (§47). Bid vs mid: pricing each bucket at its bid instead of the mid, the §41 artefact. Outside vs in-quote: a model from the free hourly price and its recent volatility against the market at its most favourable price inside each quote; positive would be information beyond the spread. Only oil a day out clears zero; found by looking, it has a sealed test from 2026-10-12 (§48). A pre-registered test on five other daily commodity ladders passed (+0.33 a day out, §49), but the gain sits in a few stale ladders and a taker makes about nothing after fees.
